- [`8de245f`](https://github.com/3fon3fonov/exostriker/commit/8de245f0ae37a82c7d8af9d76b9e3dd4dc03cc81), 2026-11-19, *"fix in st. params for cornerplot."*
  Files: `exostriker/lib/RV_mod/functions.py` (+/-60), `exostriker/lib/UI/cornerplot_stellar_options.ui` (new, +110), `exostriker/lib/cornerplot_stellar_options_ui.py` (new, +74), `exostriker/lib/cornerplot_parameter_window.py` (+/-32), `exostriker/lib/es.py` (+/-130), `exostriker/lib/UI/es.ui` (+/-2164, mostly re-serialisation), `exostriker/lib/UI/command` (+2).
  What it changes for a user:
  - In the cornerplot parameter dialog, the **incl. mass / incl. semi-major axis / incl. radius** checkboxes and the **Earth / Jupiter / Solar** units now live in their own panel (same labels). A new note at the bottom of the dialog and new tooltips explain where the stellar inputs come from (`cornerplot_stellar_options.ui:53,81-87`).
  - The derived stellar mass and semi-major axis in the cornerplot now use, in order: a sampled M★, else the **RV scatter calculator** M★ if its σ is nonzero, else the model mass and errors (`RV_mod/functions.py:1539-1553`, `_corner_mass_draws`). Radius follows the existing **Planet-radius R★ source** setting (`functions.py:1513-1536`; label at `es.py:98324`).
  - From Python, `cornerplot()` accepts new keywords `stellar_mass`, `stellar_mass_err`, `stellar_radius`, `stellar_radius_err` (`functions.py:~1625`).
  - The stellar-rotation and RV-scatter calculators now read the calculator's R★ and M★ instead of the model values (`functions.py:~2339-2370`).
  - Main window: the period-maximum spin boxes (`P_max_1..6`) can now go up to 10 000 000 instead of 100 000 (default still 100 000, `es.py:10807-10808`); the mass Jeffreys-prior alpha default changes from 0.001 to 0.0 (`es.py:12674` and the matching slots).
  - The rest of the `es.ui` diff is Qt Designer re-serialisation: no label, title or tooltip text changed (only `"` → `&quot;` escaping).
  The commit message gives the reason only as "fix in st. params for cornerplot"; the in-app note itself says "These inputs propagate uncertainties. "Derive ρ★ from M★ and R★" is not required." (`cornerplot_stellar_options.ui:87`). No other reasons are stated.

**Manual (3fon3fonov/exostriker-manual): no new commits** (still `e28df8d`).
